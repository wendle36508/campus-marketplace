# {{APP_NAME}} Community Guidelines

Everyone here signed in with a {{SCHOOL_NAME}} email and told us they're a current student. We don't check IDs. These rules keep {{APP_NAME}} a good place to buy and sell. Breaking them can get your posts removed or your account banned. {{APP_NAME}} is an independent student project, not affiliated with or endorsed by {{SCHOOL_NAME}}.

## The basics

- **Personal items only.** Sell things you own and have with you. No businesses, bulk reselling or dropshipping.
- **Be honest.** Use photos you took. Describe flaws. Mark items sold once they're gone.
- **Be respectful.** Treat people the way you'd want to be treated at a meetup.
- **Keep chats in the app.** You don't need to share your phone number.
- **Follow through.** If your plans change, tell the other person.

## Items you can't post

- **Tickets** of any kind, for now.
- **Rides**, offered or requested, including for "gas money."
- **Services** of any kind, for now. That includes haircuts, nails, lashes, tattoos and anything else that needs a license.
- **Course materials:** notes, exams, quizzes, slides, solutions, completed assignments, or a professor's course content. Used printed textbooks are fine.
- **Housing:** sublets and room listings, for now.
- **Weapons:** guns of any type (including airsoft and BB guns), ammunition, knives other than kitchen knives, pepper spray, and other weapons.
- **Fireworks** and explosives.
- **Alcohol.** Empty glassware is fine.
- **Nicotine and tobacco:** vapes, pods, e-cigarettes, cigarettes and nicotine pouches.
- **Drugs:** cannabis, CBD, any other drug, and drug paraphernalia.
- **Health products:** prescription drugs, medical devices and supplements.
- **Meal swipes, dining dollars and parking permits.**
- **Anything that belongs to {{SCHOOL_NAME}}**, like dorm furniture, signs or equipment.
- **Stolen goods**, **counterfeits or "replicas,"** and **fake IDs.**
- **Recalled products.** Check cpsc.gov/recalls before you post.
- **Gift cards, accounts, logins, subscriptions and access codes.**
- **Animals** of any kind.
- **Homemade food.**
- **Sexual content or services.**
- **Anything else that's illegal** to sell or own in Massachusetts.

The app checks listings for banned items automatically and may hide one until we review it. Not sure about an item? Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} before you post.

## Behavior that's not allowed

- Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discrimination.
- Scams. That means fake listings, bait-and-switch, asking for money or a deposit before meeting, fake payment screenshots, or asking to be paid in gift cards.
- Sharing someone else's personal information (like their address, phone number or photos) without their OK.
- Fake accounts, using someone else's account, or sharing sign-in codes.
- Pretending to be someone else, including our team or {{SCHOOL_NAME}} staff.
- Spam, like posting the same item over and over.
- Getting around a ban or a block.
- Bots, scraping, or trying to break into the app.
- Any sexual content involving minors. We take it down, keep it locked away as federal law requires, close the account, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.

## Meeting up

These are tips, not guarantees. We can't check people or items, and we don't supervise meetups.

- Meet in a busy public place on campus, in daylight if you can. The app suggests public meetup spots.
- Bring a friend, or tell one where you're going.
- Look over the item before you pay. Never pay or send a deposit in advance.
- Never share bank, card or login details, or a sign-in code.
- If something feels off, leave, and report it.

## If something goes wrong

- **In danger? Call 911 or {{SCHOOL_NAME}} Public Safety at 781-239-5555.**
- **A crime, like theft or a threat?** Report it to {{SCHOOL_NAME}} Public Safety or the police, and report it in the app too.
- **Harassment?** Report it in the app. For sexual harassment, you can also contact {{SCHOOL_NAME}}'s Title IX office. That office handles sexual harassment and other sex-based discrimination.
- **Report button:** use it on a listing or a profile, or from the ••• menu in a chat. We aim to look at reports within 24 hours, but nobody watches the app around the clock. We never tell the person you report who reported them.
- **Block button:** you stop seeing each other's listings, and they can't message you.

## What happens when rules are broken

- We may remove posts, warn you, or suspend or ban your account, at our discretion.
- When we learn someone's safety is at risk, it becomes our top priority, and we can hide listings and ban accounts quickly. Nobody watches the app around the clock, so if you're in danger, call 911 or Public Safety.
- Banned members can't make a new account. We keep their school email address to stop that.
- We close the accounts of people who repeatedly post work that isn't theirs.
- Think we got it wrong? Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
