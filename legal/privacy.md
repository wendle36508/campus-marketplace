# {{APP_NAME}} Privacy Policy

**The short version**
- Only people who sign in with a {{SCHOOL_NAME}} email and say they're current students can use the app. We don't check IDs.
- Other members never see your email address.
- We don't sell your information or show ads. We don't use trackers that follow you around the web.
- Email us and we delete your account within 7 days.

## Who runs the app

{{APP_NAME}} is an independent student project run by {{OPERATOR_NAME}} in Massachusetts. It is **not affiliated with or endorsed by {{SCHOOL_NAME}}**. {{SCHOOL_NAME}} doesn't run the app. We don't give the school access to your account or messages, except in the cases listed under "When we share information with Public Safety or police." Emails we send to your {{SCHOOL_NAME}} address are stored by {{SCHOOL_NAME}}'s email system, like any other email you get there. Privacy questions go to {{OPERATOR_NAME}} at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## What we collect

**Information you give us**
- Your {{SCHOOL_NAME}} email address.
- Your first name, last initial and graduation year, plus a profile photo if you add one.
- What you tell us when you agree to our terms: that you're a current student, whether you're 18 or older, and which version you agreed to and when.
- Your listings: photos, title, description, price, category, condition and pickup spot.
- Your chat messages, including meetup plans.
- Reports you make, students you block, and thumbs up or down you give.
- When you mark an item sold, the buyer you choose.
- Anything you send to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

**Information created as you use the app**
- Your join date, when you were last active, and whether your account is banned (and why).
- An activity log: when you sign in, post, edit, message, mark an item sold, and similar actions. It never includes the text of your messages or what you search for.
- How you found us, such as the invite or referral code you used.
- Whether someone from our team helped you post a listing, so we count our own help honestly.
- How many AI suggestions you've used, and the suggestion saved with each listing you post.
- Sign-in records: the email address you entered, scrambled copies of sign-in codes and links, and when they were sent and used.
- Your internet (IP) address. Our host's logs record it, and the app may use it for a short time to slow down sign-in abuse. We don't save it in our database.

**What we don't ask for:** passwords, payment or bank details, phone numbers, home addresses, ID cards or your location. If you type these into a chat or listing, we store them like anything else you post, so please don't. The same goes for location data hidden in a photo you upload.

**Photo location data:** before a photo uploads, the app usually shrinks it on your phone, which removes hidden location data. This doesn't always happen. GIFs keep it, and so do photos your browser can't process. To be sure, turn off location tagging in your camera settings.

## How we use it

- **To run the marketplace:** show listings, deliver messages, send sign-in emails, and check that members have a {{SCHOOL_NAME}} email.
- **To protect members:** handle reports, enforce our rules, and stop spam and abuse. The app automatically checks listing text for banned items, and may hide a listing until an admin reviews it. It also checks chat messages for phone numbers or email addresses so it can show you a safety tip. Nothing about that check is saved or reported.
- **For AI suggestions:** to suggest listing details and a price.
- **To improve the app:** see what's working (like how many listings get a message within 2 days), fix bugs, and answer your questions.

We may share totals that don't identify anyone with {{SCHOOL_NAME}} offices, mentors, judges or investors (for example, "200 students posted 400 listings"). When we present the app, we may show example listings without the seller's name. We'll pick photos that don't show anyone's face or room. We don't sell or rent your personal information, we don't show ads, and we don't use AI to read your chats.

## Who can see what

- **Other members** (anyone signed in) can see your first name and last initial, graduation year, profile photo, join date, thumbs up and down totals, number of items sold, whether your account is banned, your listings, and messages you send them. If you open a chat about a listing, its seller can see you're interested, even before you send a message. They never see your email address.
- **Admins** can see everything members see. They can also see your email address, your account status and ban reason, reports by or about you, removed or flagged listings, and who bought what. Admins are {{OPERATOR_NAME}} and anyone they choose to help handle reports. Each must first agree in writing to keep student information private.
- **People who aren't signed in** see nothing about you right now. See "Features on the way" below.
- We don't tell anyone when you block them. We never tell students who reported them.
- **Messages:** in the app, only the two people in a chat can see it. When someone reports a conversation (by reporting the other student from inside the chat), an admin may read that conversation to investigate. Messages are stored on our host Render's servers. We and Render could technically read them, but we look only to keep the app working or for the reasons on this page.

## Features on the way

- **Share links.** We're building a way to share a listing's link, for example in a group chat. Until it launches, people who aren't signed in can't see any listings, profiles, photos or chats. Once it launches, anyone with a listing's link will see a preview card with the cover photo, title, price, condition and the words "verified student." The card will never show your name, and messaging the seller will still require signing in. We'll tell you in the app or by email before it launches.
- **Alert email.** We plan to let you add a personal email address for message alerts. If you add one, we'll use it only for alerts and other members won't see it. You can remove it at any time.

## Services that handle data for us

- **Render** (United States) hosts the app and its database, so it holds everything on this page. Its server logs can include your IP address.
- **Brevo** (European Union) **or Resend** (United States) sends our emails. We use one at a time. It gets your email address and the emails we send you, like sign-in codes and links. It doesn't track whether you personally open or click our emails.
- **Anthropic** (United States) makes the AI suggestions. When suggestions are on and you add the first photo to a new listing (even if you don't finish posting it), Anthropic gets that photo, its file type, and a short instruction that names your school. It never gets your name, email, listing text or messages. Anthropic's commercial terms say it doesn't train its AI on this data. It deletes the data within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage rules or because the law requires it.
- **UptimeRobot** (Slovakia) checks that the site is up. It gets no student information.
- **Our support inbox provider** stores emails you send to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and our replies.

## When we share information with Public Safety or police

- **When you ask us to.** Say you report a theft, threat or other crime. We can send what you reported to {{SCHOOL_NAME}} Public Safety or the police, along with the listing and chat you were part of. We'll check with you first. A report alone doesn't let us give them the other student's email or account details. For that they need a legal request, unless it's an emergency.
- **When the law requires it.** We share information when we receive a valid legal request, like a subpoena, court order or search warrant. We share only what the request covers.
- **In an emergency.** Sometimes we believe in good faith that someone is in danger of death or serious injury. Then we may share what's needed with police or emergency services without waiting for a legal request.
- **Child sexual exploitation.** We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, as federal law requires.

Apart from the cases above, we don't give {{SCHOOL_NAME}} or its offices access to your account or messages. **If you're in danger, call 911 or {{SCHOOL_NAME}} Public Safety at 781-239-5555.**

## How long we keep it

- **Your account, profile, listings, photos and messages:** while your account is open. A listing you delete disappears from browsing and from your profile, but people who already chatted with you about it can still see it in that chat. We keep a hidden copy in case it's reported, until you delete your account.
- **Sign-in records:** until you delete your account. If you ask for a sign-in code but never finish signing up, we delete the email address within 30 days. You stay signed in for up to 30 days at a time.
- **Activity log and last-active time:** up to 12 months, then deleted or reduced to totals. **How you found us, the "helped post" note, AI usage counts and your terms agreement:** while your account is open.
- **Reports:** 2 years after they're filed. **Ban records** (email and reason): as long as the ban lasts.
- **Anything tied to an open report or a legal request:** until it's resolved. **Child sexual abuse material we report:** locked away for 1 year after the report, or longer if law enforcement asks.
- **Server logs, email records and Anthropic:** up to 30 days.
- **Backups:** our host's backups last up to 7 days. Any backup or other file we download that identifies students is deleted within 30 days.

## Your choices

- **Edit your profile** (name, year and photo) at any time. To remove your photo without adding a new one, email us.
- **Delete a listing** at any time.
- **Block** a student. You stop seeing each other's listings and they can't message you.
- **Delete your account:** email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your {{SCHOOL_NAME}} email address. If you can't use that address anymore, email us from another one and we'll check it's really you first.
  - Within 7 days we delete your profile, profile photo, listings and their photos, sign-in records, blocks, activity log, terms agreement, how you found us and your AI usage counts. We email you when it's done.
  - Messages you sent stay in the other student's chat, shown as from "Deleted student." Thumbs you gave stay in other students' totals, but they're no longer linked to you.
  - We keep reports, ban records, anything tied to an open report or a legal request, and anything the law requires us to keep. Copies in backups and service logs last until they expire (see above).
- **Get a copy of your information:** email us and we'll send it within 30 days.
- **Emails:** you need sign-in emails to use the app. Any email from us that isn't about your account or your activity will have an unsubscribe link.

## Cookies and tracking

We use one cookie, only to keep you signed in, for up to 30 days. While you sign in, your browser tab also remembers your email address until you close the tab. We don't use ads, analytics companies or trackers that follow you to other sites. That means no other company collects information about your activity across sites through our app, so a browser's "Do Not Track" signal doesn't change anything here.

## Security

The app uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our host says it encrypts the data it stores. There are no passwords to steal. Sign-in codes expire after 15 minutes, and we store them only in scrambled form. Only a few admins can see email addresses. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll email the students affected as soon as we reasonably can.

## Age

The app is not for anyone under 13. If we learn a child under 13 has an account, we delete it and its information. Members must be at least 17. If you're under 18, you can ask us to remove anything you posted, including messages you sent. We'll hide it from other members. We may keep a copy if it's part of a report or the law requires it. Removal may not reach copies other people made.

## If a company takes over

If we form a company to run the app, your information moves to it. We'll tell you first, and this policy will still apply.

## Changes to this policy

The date and version at the top show which version you're reading. Each version takes effect on its "Last updated" date. For important changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email, and show you the new version the next time you open the app. Small fixes get a new date.
